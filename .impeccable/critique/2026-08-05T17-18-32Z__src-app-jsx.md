### [P1] The sprint tracker's arithmetic understates you by more than half, and five of six cards contradict themselves

**What:** Three failures compounding.

1. **The headline is wrong maths.** `overallPct` (Learning.jsx:147-149) is `(completedCourses + doneMilestones) / (COURSES.length + totalMilestones)` = `(1 + 0) / (6 + 5)` = **9%**. It discards every `pct` value in the file. Weighted by course hours the honest figure is ~21%; a flat average of the `pct` values is ~25%. You are publishing a number less than half the truth, at `text-3xl`, in the largest type in the section.
2. **Badges contradict their own bars.** `n8n` is `status: "completed"` with `pct: 41` (Learning.jsx:16-17). `traversy` is `"upcoming"` with `pct: 47` (:32-33). Also `core` (7%), `prod` (30%), `coder` (14%) — all labelled "Upcoming" while showing real progress. But the live post at `posts.js:97` says *"**Eight weeks.** Six AI engineering courses… **Three** production projects."* Eight weeks from late June 2026 expired last week. A recruiter reads a self-imposed deadline, then a tracker showing 9%.

**Why it matters:** This section's job was to prove momentum. It renders as a wall of zeros — 9% · 0/4 Projects Live · 0/5 Milestones Hit — and the zeros aren't even accurate.

**Fix:** Make `overallPct` hours-weighted from `pct`. Enforce the invariant that `completed` ⇒ `pct: 100` and `upcoming` ⇒ `pct: 0`, so anything between is `in-progress`. Replace the three-zero stat row with hours completed (~26 of 120.5) — momentum, not paralysis. Edit `posts.js:97` to drop "Eight weeks" and reconcile "Three" against four projects.
